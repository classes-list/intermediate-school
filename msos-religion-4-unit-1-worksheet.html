Q1 · Blest Are We Unit 1

The Goodness of God & the Ten Commandments

Blest Are We Chapters 1, 3, 4 · Grade 4 Religion Worksheet
Essential Question: In what ways am I a steward of God's creation?

📚 Vocabulary

Read each term and its definition. Highlight or circle any that are new to you.
Steward
A person who takes care of something entrusted to them. We are stewards of God's creation.
Genesis
The first book of the Bible; it tells us about God creating the world.
Creation
Everything that God made — the sky, sea, land, plants, animals, and people.
Ten Commandments
God's rules for loving him and loving one another, given to Moses on Mount Sinai.
Covenant
A sacred promise between God and his people.
Praise
A prayer that tells God how great and good he is.
Thanksgiving
A prayer that thanks God for his gifts.
Mass
The Catholic celebration of the Eucharist, our greatest prayer.

❓ Chapter Comprehension

Chapter 1 — God Creates the World

  1. Who created the world and everything in it?
  2. Name three things God created in the beginning.
  3. How can we take care of God's creation this week? Give one concrete example.

Chapter 3 — God's Loving Care

  1. What is a covenant? Who made the first covenant with God?
  2. How does God show his loving care for us every day?
  3. Write one prayer of praise to thank God for a specific gift in your life.

Chapter 4 — The Ten Commandments

  1. To whom did God give the Ten Commandments?
  2. The first three commandments are about our relationship with ___________.
  3. The other seven commandments are about our relationship with ___________.
  4. Choose ONE commandment and describe how a 4th grader can live it out at school.

📖 Scripture Reflection

Genesis 1:31
“God looked at everything he had made, and found it very good.”
What is one part of God's creation you are most thankful for? Draw or write about it below.

🙏 Prayers to Learn
