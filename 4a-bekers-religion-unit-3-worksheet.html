The Church & Social Justice

Blest Are We Chapters 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 20 · Grade 4 Religion Worksheet
Essential Question: How can I fully experience Christ's presence — through Sacraments? Through the Church's mission?

📚 Vocabulary

Read each term and its definition. Highlight or circle any that are new to you.
Sacrament
A visible sign given by Jesus that gives us God's grace.
Social justice
Working to make sure every person is treated fairly and with dignity.
Reconciliation
The sacrament of God's forgiveness; also called Confession or Penance.
Vocation
God's call to serve him in a special way (marriage, single life, ordained, religious).
Lent
The 40 days of prayer, fasting, and almsgiving before Easter.
Triduum
The three holiest days of the year: Holy Thursday, Good Friday, and the Easter Vigil.
Catholic Schools Week
A national celebration of Catholic education (usually late January).
Almsgiving
Giving to those in need, especially during Lent.

🙏 Prayers to Learn

Read each prayer aloud. Copy it in the space below to help commit it to memory.

Act of Contrition
My God, I am sorry for my sins with all my heart. In choosing to do wrong and failing to do good, I have sinned against you whom I should love above all things. I firmly intend, with your help, to do penance, to sin no more, and to avoid whatever leads me to sin. Our Savior Jesus Christ suffered and died for us. In his name, my God, have mercy. Amen.
Vocation Prayer
Loving God, you have created me for a purpose. Help me to listen for your voice and to say yes to the vocation you have planned for me — whether it is to marriage, single life, religious life, or the priesthood. Give me the courage to follow you. Amen.
