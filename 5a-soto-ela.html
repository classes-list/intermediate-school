English Language Arts — Grade 5

Developing reading, writing, speaking, and listening skills through classic and modern literature, grammar, vocabulary, and creative expression.

🌟 Mary, Star of the Sea School Mission

Nurture, Unite, Inspire. We nurture each child’s curiosity, unite our classroom in respect and collaboration, and inspire every student to grow in knowledge, faith, and wonder for the world around them.

📚 Core Novels by Quarter

Q1 · Novel 1
🦍
The One and Only Ivan
by Katherine Applegate
A Newbery Medal winner told from the perspective of a gorilla in a shopping-mall enclosure who discovers his purpose as an artist and protector.
Focus: Character voice; first-person narration; theme; figurative language; friendship and empathy.
Q2 · Novel 2
👤
Who Was...? Series
by Various Authors
Students are assigned a different historical person to research and read about, then present their findings as biographical reports.
Focus: Biography; nonfiction text features; note-taking; summarizing; oral presentation.
Q3 · Novel 3
🌳
Tuck Everlasting
by Natalie Babbitt
A young girl meets a family who has drunk from a spring granting eternal life — and must decide what she believes about living forever.
Focus: Symbolism; imagery; theme; moral dilemmas; close reading; discussion.
Q4 · Novel 4
🌌
A Wrinkle in Time
by Madeleine L’Engle
Meg Murry travels across dimensions with her brother and a friend to rescue her scientist father from a cosmic evil.
Focus: Science fiction/fantasy genre; setting; plot structure; courage and identity; literary analysis essay.
