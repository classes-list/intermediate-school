Grade 5 Syllabus · SY 2026–2027

📖 English Language Arts

Reading · Writing · Grammar · Vocabulary

👋 Welcome

Welcome to Grade 5! This syllabus is intended to help you, the student, develop a greater understanding of what we will cover, what is expected of you, and what you can expect from me. For English, my primary focus will be to complete this year with all students developing a love for, and a grade-level understanding of, books, writing, language, and grammar.

🎯 Course Overview

This course focuses on developing reading, writing, speaking, and listening skills. Students will engage with classic and modern literature, explore grammar and vocabulary, and practice various forms of writing.

Students will complete school-wide STAR Standardized Testing at the beginning, middle, and end of the school year to monitor their overall academic progress.

📚 Class Novels

This school year, students will read four core novels as a class, along with supplemental materials to be provided. Some are classic novels that parents might recognize; others are likely unfamiliar. Novels will be provided by the school unless otherwise specified, and will align with the different literacy lessons taught each quarter.

📝 Homework Policy

Students are expected to read 20 minutes every day. In addition to the reading, other homework will be assigned. Your child’s teachers will work together to ensure that students receive a balanced homework regimen that fosters a positive learning environment and nurtures their intellectual growth.

Assignments are expected to be written daily in a student’s planner and available on Google Calendar. Parents are expected to check the student’s planner daily, support their child in time management, and follow through.

Late work will be accepted; however, late assignments may negatively affect students’ participation grades.

🤝 Classroom Expectations

  • Students are expected to come to class prepared and ready to learn.
  • Homework should be completed daily and turned in. If there is a reason the homework cannot be completed on time, the teacher should be notified in advance, and a plan should be made to complete the assignment.
  • Students are expected to always try their best and participate in class. Further, they are expected to ask questions, request clarification, and communicate via office hours or email as needed.
  • Students are expected to respect themselves, respect others, and respect all property.

Class Incentives: Individual Treasure Box visit · class community rewards.

Class Consequences:

  1. Verbal reminder
  2. One-on-one discussion, written self-reflection
  3. Parent contact
