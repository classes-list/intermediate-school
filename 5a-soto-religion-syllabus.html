🎯 Religion Course Overview

Students in Grade 5 Religion at MSOS will grow in their relationship with God and gain an understanding of the importance of each individual as part of the Church’s mission. Through Scripture, prayer, and reflection on the Sacraments and the Apostles Creed, students deepen their faith while learning how to live as disciples of Christ in their daily lives.

📝 Homework Policy

As per school policy, students are expected to allocate up to 50 minutes of homework per night. Your child’s 5th-grade teachers will work together to ensure that students receive a balanced homework regimen that fosters a positive learning environment and nurtures their intellectual growth. Students are expected to record assignments daily in their school planner. Homework and important reminders will also be posted on Google Classroom for students and families.

Parents are expected to check the student’s planner daily, support their child in time management, and follow through.

Late work will be accepted; however, submitting assignments on time is an important part of developing responsibility. Late submissions may negatively affect a student’s participation grade.

🤝 Classroom Expectations

  • Students come to class prepared and ready to learn each day.
  • Homework should be completed on time. If circumstances prevent completion, students should communicate with the teacher in advance so that an appropriate plan can be made.
  • Everyone is encouraged to participate by asking questions, contributing ideas, and seeking clarification from the teacher when needed.
  • We will treat ourselves, one another, and school property with respect and responsibility.

Class Incentives: Individual Treasure Box visit · class community rewards.

Class Consequences:

  1. Verbal reminder
  2. One-on-one discussion, written self-reflection
  3. Parent contact

🎓 Academic Integrity

Honesty and integrity are essential values in our classroom.

Teachers structure conditions during testing to alleviate the possibility of cheating; specify the types of collaboration that are discouraged and encouraged; and teach summarization vs. copying straight from the text.

Students avoid situations that might contribute to cheating or plagiarism, avoid unauthorized assistance, and use sources in the prescribed manner outlined by the teacher.

🕘 Absences & Tardiness

Students may remain at home in the case of illness, accident, quarantine, or because of a death in the family. For prolonged absences, families must notify the teacher so a plan can be developed to complete missing work.

Students are marked tardy after the 7:45 a.m. bell. Explanatory notes from parents and conferences with parents may be advisable.

🍎 Food & Drink

  • Food is prohibited in the classroom unless otherwise specified by the teacher.
  • Sharing food is not permitted to ensure adherence to dietary restrictions.

📱 Technology

To minimize distractions and maximize learning, communication with students during the school day should be limited to emergencies. Student cell phones and smartwatches (Apple Watch, Gizmo, FiLIP) must remain off and in backpacks for the duration of the school day.

Violations: 1st — Warning; 2nd — Phone sent to office for pickup from Principal; 3rd — Phone sent to office, parent notified.

iPad usage is limited to classroom assignments; school iPads do not go home.
