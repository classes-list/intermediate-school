Holy Spirit · Living Our Faith · Works of Mercy & Beatitudes

Blest Are We Chapters 7, 19, 9, 10, 11 · Grade 4 Religion Worksheet
Essential Question: How am I showing my commitment to faith by living the New Commandment?

📚 Vocabulary

Read each term and its definition. Highlight or circle any that are new to you.
Holy Spirit
The third Person of the Holy Trinity; God's presence and power in our lives.
Pentecost
The day the Holy Spirit came down on the disciples; the birthday of the Church.
Gifts of the Holy Spirit
Wisdom, Understanding, Counsel, Fortitude, Knowledge, Piety, and Fear of the Lord.
New Commandment
'Love one another as I have loved you' (John 13:34).
Corporal Works of Mercy
Actions we take to care for people's bodies (feed the hungry, shelter the homeless, etc.).
Spiritual Works of Mercy
Actions we take to care for people's spirits (comfort the sorrowful, forgive, etc.).
Beatitudes
Jesus' teachings about who is truly blessed, given in the Sermon on the Mount.
Rosary
A prayer that reflects on the mysteries of Jesus and Mary using beads.

❓ Chapter Comprehension

Chapter 7 — The Holy Spirit

  1. Who is the Holy Spirit?
  2. What happened at Pentecost?
  3. Name three of the seven Gifts of the Holy Spirit.
  4. Which gift do you feel you need most this month?

Chapter 19 — Living Our Faith

  1. What is the New Commandment?
  2. Give one example of how you can love a classmate 'as Jesus loves.'
  3. Why is loving others sometimes hard? How does the Holy Spirit help?

Chapters 9-10 — Works of Mercy

  1. Name TWO Corporal Works of Mercy.
  2. Name TWO Spiritual Works of Mercy.
  3. Pick one work of mercy and describe a project or action you can do at MSOS to live it out.

Chapter 11 — The Beatitudes

  1. Who taught the Beatitudes and where?
  2. Complete: 'Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called ___________.'
  3. Choose your favorite Beatitude and explain what it means in your own words.

📖 Scripture Reflection

John 13:34
“I give you a new commandment: love one another. As I have loved you, so you also should love one another.”
Write about a time you had to choose to love someone even when it was hard. What did you do — or what will you do next time?

Reflect on The Lorax
  1. In The Lorax, who speaks up for the trees and why?
  2. The Beatitudes call us to be peacemakers and to care for those who cannot care for themselves. How is the Lorax like a peacemaker?
